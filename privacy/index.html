Privacy Policy

Mahjong Score Keeper

Effective date: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Mahjong Score Keeper handles information when you use the app. Mahjong Score Keeper is a score-keeping application for Mahjong games. You can use the app without creating an account.

2. Information You Enter

The app allows you to enter player names, scores, and game information needed to keep track of Mahjong rounds. This information is used to provide the score-keeping functionality.

Player names and game scores may be stored locally on your device when needed for the app's functionality. The app does not require you to create an online account to use its core score-keeping features.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

Mahjong Score Keeper uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app activity, and other information permitted by applicable law and your privacy choices.

Advertising data processing is subject to Google's policies and the consent choices available to you. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Mahjong Score Keeper uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage consent for advertising and related data processing.

Depending on your location and applicable privacy requirements, you may be shown a consent message when you use the app. You may also be able to review or change your privacy choices through the privacy options provided by the app.

5. How Information Is Used

Information entered into the app is used to calculate, display, and maintain Mahjong scores and game progress.

Information processed by advertising providers is used for purposes described by those providers and permitted by applicable law and your consent choices.

6. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell the player names or game scores you enter into the app. Advertising services, including Google AdMob, may receive or process information necessary to provide advertising and related services.

Third-party services process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Game information stored locally remains on your device until it is removed by the app, cleared by you, or otherwise deleted through normal device or app management.

Information processed by third-party advertising providers is retained according to their policies and applicable legal requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Mahjong Score Keeper is not directed specifically at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an online account system.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information in a way that requires action, please contact the developer.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the app to protect information handled by Mahjong Score Keeper. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The current version will be published on this page with its effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning Mahjong Score Keeper, please contact the developer through GitHub .
